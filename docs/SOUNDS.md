# fm1-scales — The sound list

The sounds the owner picked from the sound audition (1,001 sounds of ten FM-1 firmwares, recorded by each firmware's own
code playing the same phrases; the owner's first round, 2026-11-17: 167 picks). They are what the port stories (ST-67..ST-76)
bring in, grouped by the story that brings each. The owner may drop sounds later; a sound not on this list does not ship.
Sound data from Sequential's Prophet-5 and Casio's CZ-1 banks is copied as Melodee does, with the risk accepted (#100,
D-069). Everything else is GPL-3.0 (the firmwares' own sounds) or CC0 (samples).
