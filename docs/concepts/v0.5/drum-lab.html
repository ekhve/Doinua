FM-1 Drum Lab

The drum track's three pages, playable. PLAY: the keys are the kit's pads (four kits). IDEAS: a style's beat falls onto the pads. AUTO: hold a pad and a beat plays. POCKET is the feel: it moves each drum a little off the grid, as a drummer or a Dilla beat does, and you can see it on the screen. Click Start sound first. Keys: click them, or type a to ' from C4.

Knobs: click the left or right half, or scroll. SCL: the page. PLAY, REC (a count-in when stopped). The kits are made with the synth here; the firmware's sampled kits come from open-licence recordings.
