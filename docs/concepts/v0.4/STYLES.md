# Styles and progressions — draft for the owner

**Status:** draft (2026-11-08), for the owner to shape with their artists. Today the chord pad has the simulator's starter
list (D-034): Free, Pop, Neo soul, Hip hop, West coast, Funk, Electronic, Jazz, three progressions each. This proposes
groups built around the owner's taste, the most usual progressions first.

**How a progression is written.** Degrees of the key, with the colour each chord gets: `ii9` is the second degree with
a 9 (Dm9 in C). Minor-sounding styles are written in the minor key (`i7 VImaj7` is Am7 Fmaj7 in A minor); on the FM-1 a
progression keeps its sound in either mode by moving to the relative key (A minor's chords are C major's). Chords from
outside the key are marked † (see "Open questions"). The progressions are common property of music; the artists are
what the group is inspired by, not songs copied.

## Open questions for the owner

1. **The artists**: which belong to which group, and which groups to drop or add (Chromeo, Ghibli / Zelda, G-funk,
   boom bap, D'Angelo, West coast, JJ Cale, Clapton, Rawayana, Bad Bunny, shatta / Kybba, Enya, illmind so far).
2. **Chords from outside the key (†).** Many groovy progressions borrow one: ♭VII in a major key (Zelda's I ♭VII IV,
   the rock and blues turnaround), the major IV in a minor key (G-funk's dorian i IV), III7 (Just the two of us),
   iv in major. The pads are the key's seven chords (the owner: no borrowed chords on the upper row). Options: (a)
   leave them out (these lists only use the key's chords); (b) a progression may carry its own chord on a pad while
   it is selected; (c) a black key "borrow" colour. Recommendation: (b), shown on the pad and the screen.
3. **Longer progressions**: the 12-bar and 5-chord ones need 8 steps (the pad holds 4 today); fine to raise.
