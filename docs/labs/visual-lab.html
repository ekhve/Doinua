Visual Lab

Sit back and play: the screen answers. Press Start sound, then play the keys (or your computer keyboard: z s x d c v g b h n j m and q 2 w 3 e r 5 t 6 y). PLAY starts a slow lofi loop to play over, or just to watch. K1 changes the scene, K2 what is written over it (chords, the key, note names, or nothing), K3 the sound, K4 the vibe (tape wobble, crackle, rain). Every scene uses only what the FM-1 can draw: rectangles, circles, lines, pixels and its own text, no transparency.

K1 SCENE
K2 SHOW
K3 SOUND
K4 VIBE

The scenes

  • Window: a room at night, rain on the glass. Each note lights a window across the street; chords change the sky's colour. Lofi.
  • Bloom: the twelve notes on a circle of fifths. A note blooms where it sits; a chord draws its shape, a triangle or a square, between its notes. The key's home glows in the middle.
  • Ripples: every note drops a ring from where its key is; loud notes make wide rings. Calm water when you stop.
  • Tape: a cassette, its reels turning with the loop; your notes scroll past on the tape like a piano roll, the VU needle following the loudness.
  • Sky: five minutes of playing leaves a constellation. Each note is a star at its pitch, chords join their stars; it slowly fills the night.
  • Horizon: a sun and mountains; the mountains grow from the pitches you play, the sun pulses with the beat.

How it could live on the FM-1

  • A mode of its own ("Just play": pick a sound, play), or one button on any track that swaps the page for the scene while you play or the song plays.
  • The knobs stay the sound's most fun controls (here: SOUND and VIBE); SCENE and SHOW would be one knob each.
  • The chord, the key and the note names come from what we already have (the chord naming, Sketchpad's key from notes).
