FM-1 Controls Map

Kept up to date as we decide. Every screen of the companion firmware as it is (main, 2026-11-18, with the fixes of #102), how you get to it, what each control does there, and how you get back. Read from the code, not from the manual. At the end: what I found that could leave someone stuck or surprised, and the four buttons nobody uses yet.

The rules that hold everywhere

HOME held

The mode picker, from any screen, in any mode, even in a game or while teaching the unicorn. The one way out that always works.

HOME tap

Back to the mode's home: in Producer it closes whatever is open (the song page, the REC layer, a question); in Guided the lesson list. In a game it does nothing.

GLO tap / held

The song page and back (Producer only). Held: help for the page, gone when you let go.

PLAY

Plays and stops the song everywhere; stops a count-in.

REC tap / held

Arms recording / opens the REC layer; tap again closes it.

Asleep

The press that wakes the screen does nothing else, and is ignored until you let go.

What I found

Nothing leaves you stuck: HOME held works from every screen, every question has an answer button and "any other button" as no, and the sleeping screen eats the waking press. What could surprise, and where each stands:

Fixed
SAVE undid on a tap. Now held, as R-147 always said; a tap shows "Hold SAVE to undo". Merged in #102 (D-071, R-243).
Fixed
The song page hid changes behind it. ALGORITHM, PRESETS and OCT now close it first, so you see what they did. Merged in #102 (R-240).
Fixed
HOME tap did not close everything. In Producer it now closes the song page, the REC layer and a question. In a game it still does nothing. Merged in #102 (R-241).
Open
EDIT does three unrelated things. Lights off (chord IDEAS only), erase (held with a key), and soon the sound pages. Fix: give the sound its own buttons (ENV, LFO, FX are free, below) and keep EDIT for editing notes.
Open
Settings that stay on without showing much. ARP on, lights off, SPEED below 100 %, MIDI IN on Follow (the screen jumps to the track a keyboard plays). Each is shown somewhere, but small. Fix: one row of small status marks on the play screen.
Fixed
Knobs and keys acted during a question. They now wait; a key let go still stops its note. Merged in #102 (R-242).
Fixed
A stuck note behind the mode picker (found while fixing): hold a key, hold HOME, let the key go while the modes show, and the note kept sounding. The picker now lets the release through. Merged in #102 (R-244, HAZ-02).
Good
The ways out hold. HOME held reaches the picker from games, teaching, lessons, help, the song page and the REC layer; leaving while teaching drops the unfinished song, as designed. The picker's confirm needs a second HOME; any other button cancels.

Four free buttons, and what they could be

FX, ENV, LFO and SEQ do nothing yet. Their stickers already say what the sound and sequencer pages need, so the next controls design could be:

ButtonProposalWhy
SEQThe sequencer view: the track's steps, and the song's patternsThe sticker says so, and you want to design it next
ENVJumps to the envelopes (F.ENV, then A.ENV)A shortcut into EDIT's pages
LFOJumps to the LFO pageA shortcut into EDIT's pages
FXPERFORM first, then the effects (ST-69)Live playing and effects in one place

EDIT for the sound (your choice) means its other jobs (lights off, erase with a key) need a new home: decided with the sequencer, as erasing belongs there.
