Unicorn Jump

The unicorn runs to each fence and waits for you. Easy: any key jumps it, and the fence's note plays the song. Key: the note's letter is over the fence and its key glows; a wrong one bumps it, two make it poop, and the poop comes round again to hop. Wings (Easy: half way through the song; Key: eight good jumps): off to the moon! Leave it waiting and it neighs, then falls asleep. A soft band plays the song's chords under your notes (K4).

MODE
Easy: any key
SPEED
slow
SONG
Twinkle Twinkle
MUSIC
pad, bass, reverb
Play: tap the keys here, or the computer keys A S D F G H J K L (white, C to D) and W E T Y U O P (black). A MIDI keyboard plugged into the computer works too, where the browser allows it.
On the FM-1 this is HOME (held) → Game. The glowing key is the key's own light; K1 to K4 are the four knobs.
Sprites are our own pixel art (white winged unicorn, wooden fence, crescent moon), drawn for the FM-1's screen. The songs are traditional, free to use.
