Guided: scales and chords

A sketch of the guided mode: five short lessons on the scale and the key you choose, each the same for a child and a grown-up, told two ways (K1). The FM-1 lights the way and waits; a wrong key is a soft bonk and nothing is lost. Each lesson done earns a star.

Play: tap the keys (F3 to G5, as the FM-1's 27), the computer keys A S D F G H J K L (white, from C4) and W E T Y U O P (black), or a MIDI keyboard where the browser allows it. A glowing key is the next one; a softly ringed key is in the scale.
The lessons (SELECT): 1 Climb the scale, up and down; 2 Find home: the FM-1 plays a little tune that stops, you play the home note it wants; 3 Chord towers: three keys glow, play them together or one by one; 4 A chord song: four chords, then the FM-1 plays your song back with a beat; 5 Make music: play anything over a gentle band, every key in the scale fits.
The two views (K1): Kids: pictures and colours, the letters big, a bird that climbs and flies home, towers with faces (happy major, sleepy minor), stars; three scales (Major, Minor, Pentatonic) and the three main chords. Grown-up: the note names spelled for the key, the scale on a staff, its degrees and its steps (W W H ...), each chord's name, numeral and recipe (major: 4 + 3 semitones), all seventeen of the FM-1's scales and all seven chords.
The theory is the firmware's own (its seventeen scales; chords built in thirds on each degree). A sketch to talk about, not the firmware yet.
