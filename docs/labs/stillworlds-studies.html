Stillworlds Studies

Drawing tests, to find a style closer to your references: full resolution (240 × 240), palettes with shading ramps that shift hue (cool shadows, warm light), ordered dithering, clusters lit from one side, rim light, mirrored water and depth by mist. The music lights them as in the first lab. Press Start sound, then PLAY and play over it (computer keys z s x d c v g b h n j m, q 2 w 3 e r 5 t 6 y).

What changed from the first lab

  • Twice the resolution: every pixel of the screen is drawn, not 2 × 2 blocks.
  • Each material has its own ramp of 4–8 colours that shifts hue (shadows go purple or blue, light goes warm), mixed with a 4 × 4 dither instead of smooth gradients.
  • Things are lit from one side: tree clusters, stones, walls, clouds with a lit underside from the low sun.
  • Water mirrors what is above it and breaks it with ripples; far forests are lighter and bluer, with mist between.

What the music does here

  • Little Skyworld: the kick lights the lamp and window, the bass glows under the island, notes send spirits round it and light flowers; it blooms the longer you play.
  • Neon Shore: the kick pulses the neon and the sun's reflection, snares flash windows, the bass warms the sun, notes skim light across the sea.
  • Whispering Pool: the kick rings the pool, the bass wakes the stones, each note lights runes on a stone and ripples the water.
  • Chords set the colour of sky and water in all three.
