Track Modes

PLAY, IDEAS and AUTO on each of the four tracks, today and as one pattern. Written 2026-11-18 from the firmware's manual.

What is uneven today. Three tracks share one pattern: PLAY you play, IDEAS a style's part falls onto the keys to learn, AUTO a pad plays a pattern. The chords track breaks it. Its IDEAS builds a chord with four knobs, and its AUTO is a one-finger chord pad. The HELP steps also differ (2 on chords, 4 on bass and melody, none on drums), and so do the knobs: FEEL is K3 on bass and melody but K4 on drums, SPEED is missing on bass, and the drums' PLAY knobs edit the sound.

Proposal. One sentence per mode, the same knob in the same place on every track, and one ladder of HELP steps. Every track then reads the same way. The music each track plays doesn't change.

Drums: TUNE, DECAY and FX of a pad move to the sound pages (EDIT), which are being designed now. GHOSTS joins FEEL as a choice (straight, ghosts, more ghosts). On PLAY, K2 and K3 stay empty, because a drum has no scale.

One HELP ladder

The same four steps on every track with notes. Each step helps more than the one before.

FreeOnly what you hold lights. No help.
KeyThe scale dim, its tonic breathing.
ChordWhile the song plays, the safe notes now light full. Chords: the next chord's keys. Bass: the root (today's "Key+root"). Melody: the chord's notes.
WhitesThe white keys play only the scale, so nothing can sound wrong.

Drums have two steps: Free, and Map (each kind of sound lit in its colour: kicks red, snares amber, hats teal).

Choices for you

  • 1 · The pattern itself. PLAY you play, IDEAS learn, AUTO one finger, with the same knob in the same place. Yes, or change it?
  • 2 · CHORDS · IDEAS becomes "learn a style's progression", like the other tracks. Today's chord builder (root, type, colour, voicing on the knobs) would go. Its job is still covered: IDEAS shows each chord with its colour on the keys, AUTO's black keys add colours, and PLAY names whatever you build by hand. Or keep the builder as a choice at the end of IDEA's list ("Build").
  • 3 · PLUS on every track: chords hold (latch) or +bass (the root below), bass octave or fifth, drums repeat. Melody's stays as it is.
  • 4 · The drums' TUNE, DECAY and FX move to EDIT (the sound pages), so PLAY is for playing on every track.
  • 5 · SPEED on bass IDEAS too, and FOLLOW on drums AUTO as the fills.

Once you've answered, I'll update the Controls Map and the manual, then write the changes as requirements for your approval before building. Your recorded songs aren't touched.
