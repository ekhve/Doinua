Hiru Damatxo

A sketch for a second game: the three little ladies of Donostia dance on La Concha's promenade, a jump for each note you play, in the dance costume: white blouse, black bodice, red skirt with black bands, laced stockings and abarkak. They wait for you. At the end of each line all three jump together; at the very end they turn, and the bay lights up with fireworks.

MODE
Easy: any key
MUSIC
soft chords on
Play: tap the keys, the computer keys A S D F G H J K L (white) and W E T Y U O P (black), or a MIDI keyboard where the browser allows it. In Easy any key dances the next note; in Key the next note's letter floats over the lady who dances it and its key glows. A wrong key in Key: she falls on her bottom and the other two giggle; two in a row: a seagull flies in and poops on her head.
The tune is the traditional Donostiako hiru damatxo as the old songbooks have it (Santesteban, Etxeberria-Guimon; sung and danced by 1784): 75 notes in G minor, D4 to E♭5. Its notes come from the Bertsolaritza database's MIDI of variant A.
The look: our own pixel drawing of an evening at La Concha long ago: Igeldo a long green ridge with its old lighthouse tower, Santa Clara's round island with its blinking light, Urgull a dark profile with the Mota castle on its top; one old sailing boat at anchor, a trainera rowing across now and then, gulls, waves on the sand, clouds drifting; the white railing and its lamps; three dancers in the Basque dance costume: white blouse, black bodice, red skirt with black bands, white stockings laced in red, abarkak; a braid, a ponytail, a white headscarf. A sketch to talk about, not the firmware yet.
