Stillworlds Lab

Still pixel worlds woken by music and light, from your references and ideas. Each world is a small pixel diorama that barely moves on its own; the music lights it. Drums pulse and sparkle, the bass glows deep and slow, chords set the mood and colour, the melody casts small spells, and what you play live shines brighter than the loop. Press Start sound, then PLAY and play over it (computer keys: z s x d c v g b h n j m and q 2 w 3 e r 5 t 6 y; three keys or more held is a chord).

The four worlds

  • The Whispering Pool: a moonlit clearing, standing stones round a dark pond, fireflies. The kick pulses from the pond's middle, the bass wakes the stones, chords tint the night, your notes ripple the water and lift motes.
  • The Midnight Machine: a side street at night, a vending machine humming under a lamp, wet pavement. Drums blink the city, the bass is current in the wires, chords recolour the neon; play a phrase and the machine dispenses something that glows.
  • Miami Dusk: a beach at sunset, palms, a skyline, a neon kiosk. Chords move the hour from golden dusk to neon night, the bass breathes the sun, notes skip light on the water.
  • The Little Skyworld: a floating island with a house, a tree and a pond. It grows while you play: flowers, lit windows, spirits, then the tree glows. Play for a few minutes.

Rare things happen in each, now and then. Your live notes are brighter than the loop's.

On the FM-1

  • Drawn at half resolution (120 × 120 pixels, each a 2 × 2 block), so the world is a small picture in flash and the light is drawn in a buffer of 29 KB in RAM, then sent to the screen.
  • The loop's parts (drums, bass, chords, melody) come from the song's tracks, so the world knows which is which; your keys are "live".
  • Next worlds from your list: The Lantern Grove and The Star Shrine.
