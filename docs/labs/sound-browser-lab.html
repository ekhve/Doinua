How you find a sound among your 208 picks, sorted by type. Turn PRESETS on any track page: the sound list opens over the page on the track's type, PRESETS walks the sounds, K1 jumps to another type. Play the keys while it is open to hear the sound; it closes by itself 2 seconds after you stop turning or playing, or with HOME. Sounds marked with a dot are the first ones to be built (one of each type); the rest follow engine by engine.

How it works

  • The list is the 11 types down the left, the sounds of the type on the right (names only, twelve at a time, sounds that sound alike next to each other), the one chosen lit, its number in the type at the top ("3 / 32").
  • PRESETS turns one sound at a time; past the last sound of a type it goes on to the next type, so turning alone walks through everything.
  • K1 jumps a whole type, back to where you were in it, only while the list is open ("K1 GROUP" under the groups says so); otherwise K1 is the page's own knob.
  • Each track remembers its sound. A new BASS track opens on Bass, MELODY on Lead, CHORDS on Keys, DRUMS on Drums.
