Sound Edit Lab

EDIT: how the sound is built, a page a press. FX: PERFORM (the Kaoss pad), then the effects. ENV and LFO jump to their pages. HOME: back to playing. Turn the knobs (drag, scroll or arrows) and play the keys.

BEND
MOD

Computer keys: A–K white, W E T Y U black, Z / X octave. BEND and MOD are a MIDI keyboard's wheels.

What the keys play

As the track's page sets them: a note, a chord of C major, or the C minor pentatonic. The sound pages never change this: hold a chord and shape it.

The pages

  • EDIT: OSC (the waves), FILTER (with the live spectrum), F.ENV, A.ENV, VOICE (poly or mono, glide, slide always or legato, bend range), LFO (rate, depth, target, wave), MOD (what the mod wheel moves: two targets, each with an amount).
  • FX: PERFORM (BEND, MOD, X, Y; SELECT picks what X and Y move: FILTER, WAH, DUB, SPACE, DIRT, WOBBLE), DELAY, REVERB, DIRT.
  • ENV jumps to F.ENV, again to A.ENV; LFO to the LFO page. Every setting lives on one page only.
  • Glide works on chords: each note of a new chord slides from the nearest note of the last one.
