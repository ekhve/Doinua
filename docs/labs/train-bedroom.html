Bedroom Night Train

MODE
Easy: any key
MUSIC
chords on
Play: tap the keys, the computer keys A S D F G H J K L (white) and W E T Y U O P (black), or a MIDI keyboard where the browser allows it. Each note is a chuff: the engine moves on, a puff of smoke in the note's colour goes up, and at the end of each line it whistles choo choo. Stop playing and it slows down and waits, hissing. In Key mode the next note's letter shines over the engine and its key glows. A wrong key: the engine coughs black smoke. Two in a row: a big sooty sneeze, right over the passenger.
The tune is the traditional I've Been Working on the Railroad (printed 1894, public domain), the verse and "Dinah, won't you blow", in G as the Digital Tradition has it: 92 notes, D4 to C5.
