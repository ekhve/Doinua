What changed

  • v4: each white key plays the chord of its own step, as if in C: C = I, D = ii, E = iii, F = IV, G = V, A = vi, B = vii° (in any key). The Pads screen names the key over every chord, and every chord of the progression with the keys that play it (Am7 = A + C#).
  • v4: fixed: chords of the key were called "outside" (a bug), and a colour now shows in the chord's name (Cmaj7).
  • v4: FEEL plays like hands: tight, pianist (bass first, the rest a hair later, the top note sung), rolled, strum up, strum down.
  • v4: IDEAS are snippets: a progression with a rhythm (push, hip hop, stabs, halves), 4 to 6 a style. SELECT walks the chords and lights them, stopped too.
  • v4: AUTO: 9 players (pop piano, ballad, arp, Alberti, reggae, house, funk, neo soul, pads), SWING you can hear, SELECT picks the chord it starts on.
  • The keys' names are on the keys outside the pads.
  • v3: on Pads and AUTO, the screen draws the FM-1's keys with what each one plays (C, Dm… on the white keys; s2, s4, 6, 7, M7, 9, +9, 11, 13, ° on the black; NX, LK, AG at the top). On the other pages a small keyboard shows the lights.
  • v3: turning PROGRESSION opens a numbered menu: number, style, and the progression in numerals (ii9 V13 Imaj7).
  • The keyboard is the right way up.
  • No more breathing lights: full means play it, half means next or suggested.
  • Shapes' inversions always fit on the FM-1's 27 keys.
  • K2 TRANSPOSE on Free and Key: whites puts any major key on C's white keys, and any minor key on A's.
  • The black keys hold 10 colours, in order: sus2, sus4, 6, 7, maj7, 9, add9, 11, 13, dim.
  • NEXT and AGAIN play in the octave of your last pad.
  • FEEL is block, strum up, strum down, or human. Arp is gone from FEEL (it gets its own button).
  • PROGRESSION starts with None, Mine, Follow bass and Follow melody, then 45 progressions: our 13 styles and Sketchpad's 14 sets.
  • IDEAS: the next chord shows at half light for the whole bar before it comes. SPEED goes back to 100% when you leave IDEAS.
  • AUTO: PLAY runs the progression by itself, with a rhythm. Holding a pad changes the chord, as in GarageBand's autoplay.

PLAY · HELP

  • Free: it names what you play. K2 TRANSPOSE, K4 PLUS (hold, oct).
  • Key: the key's notes are faint and the home note half; the 7 chords are on the screen. Hold one note: the rest of a chord with it lights full; SELECT gives the next one; K3 COLOUR colours it. Let go: the usual next chords light half in the row.
  • Shapes: the chord's keys light full and the next chord's half. SELECT moves on. K2 PROGRESSION, K3 COLOUR, K4 VOICING.
  • Pads: each white key is a chord; hold a black key for a colour. F5 NEXT, G5 AGAIN, F#5 LOCK. K2 PROGRESSION, K3 VOICING, K4 FEEL.

PROGRESSION, the first four

  • None: no progression. All the key's chords are on the white keys, and NEXT plays a likely next chord.
  • Mine: the last chords you played on the pads, up to 8. Play four pads, then NEXT loops them: your own progression.
  • Follow bass: with the demo song playing, the suggested chord is the key's chord built on the bass note playing now.
  • Follow melody: the key's chord that holds the melody note playing now.

IDEAS and AUTO

  • IDEAS: press PLAY. The chord now lights full and the next one half, a whole bar ahead. The first time round you listen, then it's your turn. K1 STYLE, K2 IDEA, K3 SPEED, K4 HEAR.
  • AUTO: press PLAY. The progression plays by itself with the style's rhythm. Hold a white key to play another chord instead, and a black key to colour it. K1 STYLE, K2 PROGRESSION, K3 FEEL (straight, swing), K4 PATTERN.
