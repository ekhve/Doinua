Guided mode: five minimal screens

The FM-1 has one 240 × 240 screen and you read only it, so each variant must say clearly what to do. Every screen below is drawn at the device's size in its own font sizes (10, 13, 16, 24 and 40 px), the same seven moments in each row. Compare them, pick one, or pick parts of several.

Size:
What the four share: the lesson and the step at the top, what to do as the strongest text, green when done, red for a warning. They differ in how much else the screen shows.
Not shown here: the keys' lights, which do half of the guiding on the FM-1 (bright = play this, dim = the family).
