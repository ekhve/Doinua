Usage scenarios: try each one

On a computer, use the keyboard. It plays the FM-1 keys (the letter is on each key: the white keys on a s d f g h j k l ; ', the black ones above on w e r y u o p [, the top keys on z n x m c v , b) and holds the buttons: Shift = EDIT, Tab = LFO, Space = PLAY, Enter = REC, Backspace = SAVE (hold), ← → = PRESETS, ↑ ↓ = SELECT, − = OCT−, = = OCT+, Esc = HOME, 1–4 = K1–K4 up (Shift + 1–4 down). If a key does nothing, click an empty part of the page. On a phone or tablet, hold with two fingers.

1. Pick a kit and finger-drum

  1. PRESETS (← →): Dusty, 808, Live, Latin, then MY KIT 1, 2, 3.
  2. Play the first octave: a kick, s snare, w hat, e open hat, d clap. The screen names the key (F3 · KICK, ★ AUTO); keys above E4 show only their sound's name.
  3. PLAY: a beat on the kit, as AUTO would play it. PLAY again stops.

2. Mix fast on the PLAY page

  1. Play a key, then K1 LEVEL and K2 TUNE change that key (a kick shows the note it sounds).
  2. K3 FX picks the track's effect (Punch, MPC, Lofi, Tape, Delay, Reverb), K4 AMOUNT how much. Run the beat while you turn them.

3. Shape a sound (EDIT)

  1. EDIT: the SOUND page (the tabs at the top show SOUND · TRIM). Play G3 (snare).
  2. K1 TUNE, K2 ATTACK, K3 DECAY (a straight fade, cut as short as you like), K4 LEVEL. Turning does not play it: play it yourself.
  3. SELECT: its mute group. EDIT again: TRIM (samples only). HOME: back to playing.

4. A quiet hi-hat made louder

  1. PRESETS to MY KIT 1 (your example: a kick, a snare, a hat and a clap from the phone; dots on the keys). The hat on F#3 (w) was recorded too quiet.
  2. EDIT twice: TRIM. Play w. K4 GAIN up to MAX (as loud as it goes without clipping). Then LEVEL (on SOUND or PLAY) sets it in the beat. K1 START, K2 END, K3 REVERSE trim it.

5. Replace the kick with one from your phone

  1. Phone: 808 kick. PRESETS to MY KIT 2. LFO: the sampler, TAKE.
  2. Press ▶ on the phone: you hear it (monitor) and see its level. K2 IN LEVEL until the hits land between the two marks. Try the phone's volume at 20 %.
  3. REC: armed. It starts recording when the sound comes in (K3 START on sound), and stops on REC (or at 11 s). PLAY hears the take, with a play line.
  4. LFO: CHOP. K4 SLICES 1. Play a: the slice.
  5. REC: place. Press a (F3): you hear it there; press a again: stored. The key's settings start fresh. SAVE (hold), K1 to choose, SAVE (hold) again: kept.

6. Chop a break onto several keys

  1. Phone: Drum break. LFO to TAKE, REC, let two bars play, REC.
  2. LFO to CHOP. K4 SLICES 1, 2, 4, 8, 16 or by hits. The keys play the slices (lit, from F3 up).
  3. Play a slice's key to choose it (or SELECT). K1 START, K2 END, K3 ZOOM.
  4. OCT+ splits the slice (at the play line while it plays, else in the middle); OCT− merges it with the one before. Up to 27 slices, one a key.
  5. REC: place. SELECT moves through the slices and plays each; press a key twice to put it there. SELECT past the last: ALL (every slice from the key you press up). REC again: done.

7. Choose a sound for a key without sampling

  1. On MY KIT 2, hold a key (say k, F4) and press PRESETS (→): your samples first, then sounds of the same kind, then all. The key's settings start fresh.

8. Mute groups

  1. Dusty: play e (open hat) then w (hat): the hat cuts the open one (both in group A).
  2. EDIT, play e, SELECT to off: now they ring together.

9. GLO · USB AUDIO

  1. GLO: OUT (what goes to the phone or MPC), MONITOR (when you hear the input), IN LEVEL.

Decided

  1. The sampler is part of the DRUMS track; drums only for now. MY KIT 1–3, 7 s each, any of the 27 keys; a key not sampled keeps a synth sound (key held + PRESETS).
  2. Drum map: the 12 AUTO sounds on F3–E4, GM style; F4–G5 free. Only AUTO keys show a role name.
  3. PLAY page: K1 LEVEL and K2 TUNE of the key, K3 FX, K4 AMOUNT. PRESETS: the kit; SELECT: nothing (free).
  4. EDIT: SOUND (TUNE, ATTACK, DECAY linear, LEVEL; SELECT mute group) ↔ TRIM (START, END, REVERSE, GAIN). Knobs never play the sound.
  5. LFO: the sampler, TAKE ↔ CHOP. One take, 11 s; REC arms, it records on sound, REC stops.
  6. CHOP: keys play the slices; OCT+ split, OCT− merge; up to 27; REC to place, two presses, SELECT plays.
  7. A new sound on a key resets its settings. Controls a page does not use do nothing. SAVE held saves a kit.

USB audio, both ways

Like the MPC Sample, the FM-1 would be a USB audio device in both directions at once, with nothing to switch:

  • OUT: what you play always goes to the phone, computer or MPC in good quality (record it there). mix or 4 tracks (each track on its own pair, for stems; only if the USB audio part allows it).
  • IN: the phone, computer or MPC always arrives, ready to sample. MONITOR only says when you hear it through the FM-1: off, when sampling (on TAKE and CHOP), always (to jam along).

Not proven on the FM-1 yet: our firmware does USB MIDI today; USB audio comes from Melodee's work and needs a test on your FM-1.

4-bit IMA ADPCM at 22 kHz (Felucca's format), in Felucca's three 80 KB sample slots; kits play from flash. A synth key costs nothing. While the take is there, START and END can go anywhere in it; after, a sample only gets shorter.
