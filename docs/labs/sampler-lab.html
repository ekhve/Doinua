Usage scenarios: try each one

Keyboard: the FM-1 keys are on a w s e d r f g y h u j k o l p ; [ ' z n x m c v , b (the letter is on each key). Buttons: Space PLAY · Enter REC (hold for the REC layer) · Shift EDIT · ` FX · Tab LFO · \ SCL · Backspace SAVE (hold) · Esc HOME · ← → PRESETS · ↑ ↓ SELECT · − = OCT− OCT+ · 1–4 K1–K4 up, Shift+1–4 down. Click an empty part of the page if keys do nothing.

1. Record a beat (PLAY)

  1. PRESETS (← →) picks the kit. Enter (REC on), Space (PLAY): the bar runs. Play a (kick), s (snare), then w (hat) on the next rounds. A row a key, in the order you record them; the rows share the space, with names while they fit. With the mouse, press low on a key to play harder (the FM-1's keys feel how hard).
  2. K3 GRID (off, 1/8, 1/16, 1/32, 1/8T, 1/16T) and K4 FEEL (swing …) move the whole track on playback; what you played is kept: GRID off and it plays as played. K1 LEVEL and K2 FX: the key you played last.

2. Erase, clear, undo, redo

  1. Erase: REC on, playing, hold Shift (EDIT) and hold w. Undo: hold Backspace (SAVE), up to 4 back. Redo: two quick taps on Backspace. A single tap says HOLD: UNDO.
  2. Clear: hold Enter (REC layer), = (OCT+): it asks; = again clears.

3. Build a kit (EDIT → KIT)

  1. Shift (EDIT): KIT. Touch a key; → (PRESETS): every sound you have (your samples, then kicks, snares, hats, toms), starting at the key's own. K1 LEVEL, K2 VELOCITY (fixed, or how much it follows how hard you play).
  2. Hold Backspace: the save panel; 1 picks MY KIT 1–3; hold again: saved.

4. Shape a sound (EDIT → SOUND, TRIM)

  1. SOUND: K1 TUNE, K2 ATTACK, K3 HOLD (full: the whole sound), K4 DECAY (after HOLD), all in ms, drawn as straight lines. SELECT: mute group.
  2. TRIM (samples): on MY KIT 1 touch w (a quiet hat): K4 GAIN up to MAX.

5. Compress and clip the kit, echo the snare (FX)

  1. ` (FX): PUNCH: K1 COMP, K2 CLIP. FX again: DIRT: K1 type (Drive, Lofi, Tape, Crush), K2 amount. FX again: SPACE: K1 DELAY (off, 1/16 …), K2 FEEDBACK, K3 REVERB.
  2. Every pad goes through PUNCH and DIRT (its TRACK FX starts at 10). EDIT → PAD FX: on the hats, TRACK FX down (clean); on the snare, DELAY and REVERB up.

6. Sample a kick (LFO)

  1. Phone: 808 kick. PRESETS to MY KIT 2. Tab (LFO): TAKE. 2: IN LEVEL. Enter: armed, it records on sound; Enter: stop.
  2. Tab: CHOP, K4 SLICES 1. Enter: put mode (red). Press a: F3 · KICK: press again to store; press a again: Stored on F3. Nothing sounds; SELECT plays the slice.

7. Chop a break

  1. Phone: Drum break. TAKE, two bars. CHOP: K4 hits. The keys play the slices. = split, − merge.
  2. Enter: put mode. ↑ ↓ chooses the slice (it plays). Press a key twice. K4 PUT: all puts every slice from the key you press up.

Decided

  1. DRUMS: SCL PLAY → IDEAS → AUTO (the top line shows only the page you are on, the kit and the tempo). EDIT builds the kit (KIT · SOUND · TRIM · PAD FX); FX the track's effects (PUNCH · DIRT · SPACE); LFO the sampler; ENV free.
  2. PLAY: K1 LEVEL and K2 FX of the key played, K3 GRID, K4 FEEL. PRESETS: the kit.
  3. SAVE: held, undo (up to 4) on PLAY; two taps, redo; held in EDIT and the sampler, save the kit.
  4. Erase needs REC on, on every track.
  5. Drum map v2; MY KIT 1–3, 7 s each; one take of 11 s; OCT+/OCT− split and merge; put a slice with two presses.

Proposed in v6 (yours to confirm)

  1. GRID and FEEL for the whole track, on playback (what you played is kept), as built on every track; not per pad.
  2. Velocity per pad (KIT · K2): fixed, or 1–10.
  3. SOUND: TUNE, ATTACK, HOLD, DECAY in ms; LEVEL moves to KIT and PLAY.
  4. FX: PUNCH (COMP, CLIP), DIRT (off, Drive, Lofi, Tape, Crush), SPACE (DELAY with off, FEEDBACK, REVERB). The compressor is the track's, shared; each pad's TRACK FX says how much it gets (one compressor a pad would cost too much processing).
  5. CHOP put mode: silent; SELECT plays; K4 PUT one or all.
