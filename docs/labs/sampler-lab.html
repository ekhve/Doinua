Sampler Lab

Version 3 (2026-11-26), from our talk: the sampler lives in the DRUMS track. Every kit, built in or yours, has the same 27 keys, the drum map (kicks on the left, hats in the middle, the crash at the top), so AUTO and IDEAS always find the kick, snare and hats. A MY KIT holds your samples on any keys; the keys you have not sampled keep a synth sound. Press Start sound, then follow the usage scenarios below the FM-1. What you record goes through the 4-bit ADPCM the FM-1 would store, so what you hear is the real quality.

Decided so far

  1. The sampler is part of the DRUMS track: no SAMPLES template. PRESETS lists the built-in kits, then MY KIT 1–3. Two drum tracks (GLO TRACKS) can play two kits. Drums only for now.
  2. MY KIT 1–3, 7 s each in flash (Felucca's sample-slot size and place), on all 27 keys of the drum map; a key you have not sampled keeps a synth sound (any sound of any kit, chosen with the key held + PRESETS).
  3. EDIT on a drum track goes round SOUND → TRIM → TAKE → CHOP. A kit is saved with SAVE held, as a sound preset is (Preset Save).
  4. One take (up to 11 s) for now. REC held + a key is dropped.

To try and decide in this lab

  1. Two presses to place a slice on a key (the first plays it there and names the key; the second stores it).
  2. GAIN up to MAX on TRIM (amplify a quiet hit as far as it goes without clipping; MAX is "normalize").
  3. Mute groups A–D on SOUND (SELECT): keys in one group cut each other off. Built-in kits: only the hats share group A.
  4. Monitor on TAKE: you hear the phone and see its level before you record; IN LEVEL sets it.
  5. EDIT held + PLAY splits the chosen slice in two.

Usage scenarios: try each one

With a mouse, turn on HOLD (lab) for "held" steps: then a button or key stays held until you press it again. On a phone or tablet you can simply hold with two fingers. Knobs: drag up or down, or use − and +.

1. Pick a kit

  1. Press keys: each plays its sound; the screen names it on the drum map (F3 · KICK 1). The coloured bar on each key is its group (red kicks, amber snares and claps, teal hats, purple toms and percussion); ★ marks the keys AUTO and IDEAS play.
  2. PRESETS ◀ ▶: Dusty, 808, Live, Latin (synth), then MY KIT 1, 2, 3 (yours). PLAY runs a beat on the kit, as AUTO would: hear how each kit sounds. PLAY again stops.

1.2 Tweak a synth sound

  1. On Dusty, press EDIT: the SOUND page. Press A3 (SNARE 1).
  2. K1 TUNE, K2 DECAY, K3 FILTER (darker), K4 LEVEL; each turn plays it. The top line shows Dusty *: changed, not saved.
  3. Hold SAVE: the save panel. K1 picks where (a built-in kit cannot be overwritten, so MY KIT 1–3); hold SAVE again.

2. Play your sampled kit with AUTO

  1. PRESETS to MY KIT 1 (an example: a kick, a snare, a hat and a clap from the phone on KICK 1, SNARE 1, HAT 1 and CLAP 1; dots on the keys; the hat was recorded too quiet).
  2. PLAY: the beat plays your samples where the drum map says, and synth sounds on the other keys. That is why the place of a sample matters: put a kick on a snare key and AUTO plays it as the snare.

3. Tweak a sample: a quiet hi-hat made louder

  1. MY KIT 1, EDIT to TRIM, press F4 (HAT 1, a sample). TRIM is for samples only.
  2. K1 START and K2 END trim it (the shaded part is used). K3 REVERSE plays it backwards.
  3. K4 GAIN: up to MAX, as loud as it goes without clipping (normalize). Then LEVEL on SOUND sets how loud it sits in the beat. Is this the right split (GAIN for the sample, LEVEL for the mix)?

4. Replace the kick with one from your phone

  1. Phone: choose 808 kick. PRESETS to MY KIT 2. EDIT until TAKE.
  2. Monitor: on TAKE you hear the phone and see its level. Press ▶ on the phone; turn K2 IN LEVEL until the loudest hits reach the mark (green, not red). Try the phone's volume at 20 %: the meter says too quiet.
  3. REC, play the kick on the phone, REC again. K3 START on sound (the default) cuts the silence before it.
  4. EDIT to CHOP, K4 SLICES 1. PLAY plays it (again: from the start).
  5. REC: place mode, the drum map on the screen. Press F3 once: you hear your kick there and see F3 · KICK 1 ← slice 1. Press F3 again: stored (a dot). Another key instead moves the preview.
  6. Press F3 to play it. Hold SAVE twice (panel, then write): MY KIT 2 keeps it. Until then it is only in memory.

5. Chop a recording onto several keys

  1. Phone: Drum break. TAKE: REC, let it play two bars, REC.
  2. CHOP: K4 SLICES 1, 2, 4, 8, 16 or by hits. The keys play the slices from F3 up (lit).
  3. SELECT picks a slice; PLAY plays only that slice. K1 START and K2 END move its edges; K3 ZOOM for fine work.
  4. EDIT held + PLAY splits the slice in two (say a kick and a hat in one slice). 16 at most.
  5. Place: REC, then one slice at a time (two presses each), or SELECT past the last slice to ALL: two presses on a key put every slice from that key up.
  6. EDIT held + a key (on CHOP or TRIM) gives the key back its synth sound.

6. Choose a sound for a key without sampling

  1. On MY KIT 2, hold a key (or HOLD (lab) and press it) and press PRESETS ◀ ▶: first your samples (from any MY KIT), then the synth sounds of that key's group, then all the others.

7. Mute groups

  1. Dusty: play G#4 (OPEN 1), then F4 (HAT 1): the closed hat cuts the open one (both are in group A).
  2. EDIT to SOUND, press G#4, SELECT to off: now they ring together. Put a long sound and a short one in group B and try it.

8. GLO · USB AUDIO

  1. GLO: OUT (mix or 4 tracks), IN (off, hear: always through the FM-1, sampler: only on TAKE), IN LEVEL. With IN off, TAKE refuses to record and says why.

MY KIT 1, 2, 3Flash, kept7 s each, on any of the 27 keys

4-bit IMA ADPCM at 22 kHz, about 11 KB a second, Felucca's format. The kits sit in Felucca's three 80 KB sample slots (240 KB, 0xA0000 to 0xDC000) and play straight from flash: no RAM. A synth key costs nothing.

Trimming: while the take is still there, START and END can go anywhere in it; once the take is replaced, a sample can only be made shorter.

Later, as their own stories
