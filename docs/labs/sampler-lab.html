How to try it

  1. A kick in five seconds. On the DRUMS page with MY KIT 2 picked (SELECT), play the 808 kick on the phone, then hold REC and hold a white key: it records while you hold the key (2 s at most), cuts the silence before the sound and puts it on that pad.
  2. Chop a loop. EDIT opens the sampler pages: TAKE → CHOP → PAD → KIT (EDIT again goes round; HOME back to DRUMS). On TAKE, play the break on the phone and tap REC to start and stop (10 s at most). On CHOP, K4 SLICES picks by hits or 4/8/16 equal parts; K1 and K2 set the part of the take to use; PLAY hears it. Turn REC on and press a key: the slices go onto the pads from that key up. REC off: the keys only play, as on STEPS.
  3. Shape a pad. PAD shows the last pad you pressed: K1 TUNE, K2 ATTACK, K3 DECAY, K4 LEVEL. EDIT held + a key clears a pad.
  4. Keep it. KIT: K1 picks MY KIT 1, 2 or 3; SAVE held saves the pads there (it asks first if the kit has sounds). Then the take can go: on TAKE, SAVE held throws the selected take away and frees its RAM.
  5. GLO opens the USB AUDIO settings; with IN at off nothing records.

Sound is stored as 4-bit IMA ADPCM at 22 kHz, about 11 KB a second, the format Felucca uses for its own user samples. The kits sit in the flash area you chose (option B, 0xA0000 to 0xDC000): 240 KB, exactly Felucca's three 80 KB sample slots.

The kits play straight from flash, as Felucca's do, so a kit costs no RAM. That is what makes two kits at once possible.

Recording one pad, step by step (REC held + a key)

The fastest way from a sound on your phone to a pad, without the TAKE and CHOP pages. You are making a beat on the DRUMS page with MY KIT 2 and want your own kick on the first white key.

  1. Get the sound ready. The phone is plugged in by USB and GLO · USB AUDIO · IN is hear, so you hear the phone through the FM-1. Find the kick on the phone; don't play it yet.
  2. Hold REC. Nothing records yet. The screen's top line turns red and reads REC + KEY: RECORD A PAD; the white keys light (the 16 pads), the empty ones dim. (A short tap on REC still does what it always did: arms the song's recording. Only holding it and then pressing a key records a pad.)
  3. Still holding REC, hold the white key (C3, PAD 1). Recording starts at once; the key blinks red and a level meter and a counter (0.0 → 2.0 s) show on the screen.
  4. Play the kick on the phone, while both are held.
  5. Let go of the key (or the 2 s run out). Recording stops. The silence before the hit is cut, so the pad starts right on the kick. The FM-1 plays the new pad once and shows PAD 1 · C3: 0.42 s · SAVE held keeps it.
  6. Let go of REC. Play C3: your kick. Not happy? Do it again: it replaces the pad.
  7. Keep it: hold SAVE (on the KIT page, or SAVE held from DRUMS): MY KIT 2 is written to the flash. Until then the pad is in RAM only and is gone at power-off, as an unsaved song.

What it never does: it does not touch your takes (TAKE and CHOP stay as they were), nor the song's notes (no note goes into the sequence while REC is held). When it says no: with USB IN off ("USB IN is off: GLO"); with the kit full ("MY KIT 2 is full (7 s)"); on the factory kit, which is fixed.

To decide: keep it as it is; or make it a separate mode (REC held on DRUMS only arms pad recording, the next key press records), which is easier to explain to a child but one step longer; or drop it and record only through TAKE → CHOP.

Per-pad extras: try them

Press a key to choose a pad (its name shows here), then switch the extras on and play it. Each would be a second row on the PAD page on the FM-1 (SELECT moves to it: K1 REVERSE, K2 CHOKE, K3 LOOP), stored with the kit (a byte a pad: no flash or RAM to speak of, and no extra CPU).
