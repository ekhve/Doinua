Sequencer Lab

Version 2, from your answers of 2026-11-19. A sequence is one section of all four tracks; the song is sequences in order. Press Start sound, then PLAY. SEQ steps through the views SONG → SEQUENCE → STEPS → MOTION. A demo song with three sequences is loaded.

SONG

  • Lit keys are your sequences; dark keys are empty. Stopped: tap a lit key and it goes into the song after the cursor. Playing: it plays next, on the bar; with REC on, the order you play becomes the song.
  • Copy: hold a lit key and tap a dark one. Delete: EDIT + the key, then EDIT + the key again within 3 s (the screen asks).
  • K1 cursor, K2 repeats (drawn as dashed copies), K3 move, K4 which sequence the block plays. EDIT tapped removes the block. The song scrolls; the lanes are named by their template.
  • New song (empty) above: one empty sequence, 1, in a one-block song.

SEQUENCE

  • SELECT picks the sequence, ALGORITHM the track. REC while playing records as played; velocity from where you press the key.
  • K1 QUANTIZE (off, 1/4, 1/8, 1/8T, 1/16, 1/16T, 1/32, 1/64), K2 SWING (50–75%), K3 STRENGTH (how far notes move), K4 LENGTH. The grid lines show the quantize; a faint line shows where each note was played. Nothing is lost: turn it back and the timing is yours again.
  • SAVE held: undo the last take. EDIT + REC: clear the track (SAVE held brings it back). EDIT + key while playing: erase that note as it passes.

STEPS

  • K1 GRID (1/4 … 1/64, triplets too); the view zooms with it, 16 cells around the cursor. K2 STEP, K3 LENGTH, K4 VELOCITY.
  • REC off: the keys only sound. REC on: a key or a chord writes at the cursor. EDIT + key removes.

MOTION and FX

  • MOTION lists the lanes the track has recorded, then the knobs it could record, in real units (Hz, %, dB, octaves). K1 picks the lane, K2 is its knob: with REC on while playing, turns are recorded; again over it replaces. EDIT tapped clears the lane. In the FM-1 any knob of any page records the same way.
  • FX held (double-click to keep it open): punch-in effects on the white keys, K1 TARGET ALL or one track; recorded with REC on.
