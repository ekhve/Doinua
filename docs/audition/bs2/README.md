# Bass Something 2: the owner's 11 picks, as auditioned

The original audition recordings (round 4, 2026-11-21) of the 11 Bass Something 2 patches the owner picked, for
listening comparisons while porting the engine (WORK_ORDER task 1). Recorded from Bass Something 2's own firmware
(https://github.com/imijes/bass-something-2 at 4c8a82f, MPL-2.0), stepped with PRESETS from P01 and played from its
own keys (it takes no MIDI notes). One note at a time. The phrases are `tools/audition/phrases.py` at 96 BPM: `bass` for
the basses (a low A groove with octave jumps, ghost notes and a slide, twice, ending on a held low A), `lead` for the
leads. 12.3 s each, Opus 64 kbit/s, from the stereo recording (left channel = right channel). No effects added: the
sound is the firmware's own, its effects included.
